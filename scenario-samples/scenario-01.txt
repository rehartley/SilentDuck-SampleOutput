1956 - Black Sea Radar Barges

A Soviet radar flotilla is reportedly preparing to shadow a Turkish naval exercise near the Bosporus. A fictional civilian dredging company has hired Polish radio technicians to fit two barges with passive receivers. Moscow may regard the work as a disguised NATO listening post. Local officials are divided: Ankara wants the exercise to proceed, while a Soviet trade delegation threatens to cancel grain contracts. The central question is whether the barges are meant to collect radar characteristics or provoke a maritime incident.
